05 Payments

How billing
works.

Everything is managed from your online account. No paper forms, no phone calls, no waiting on hold.

Create your account

Sign up online with your name, email and a password. Choose your tier and enter payment details — the whole thing takes about two minutes.

Pay monthly by card

Memberships are billed monthly on the date you joined. We accept all major debit and credit cards. No direct debit mandate required.

03

Upgrade, downgrade, freeze or cancel from your account page. Changes take effect from your next billing date, with no fees or penalties.

If you're injured, travelling or just need a break, you can freeze your membership rather than cancelling it. Your tier is held, your billing stops, and you pick up exactly where you left off.

06 Questions

Membership
questions.

Everything people ask before signing up. If yours isn't here, get in touch and we'll answer it directly.

01 Is there a minimum contract?

No. Every membership is rolling, billed monthly, with no minimum term. You can cancel at any time from your account, and you won't be charged again after your next billing date.

There's also no joining fee, no exit fee and no admin charge for changing tiers.

02 Which tier should I start on?

If you're brand new to martial arts, start on Basic. Two sessions a week in one discipline is enough to build the habit without overwhelming your schedule, and you can upgrade at any point.

If you already know you want to train multiple arts, Advanced is the sweet spot — two disciplines and five sessions a week covers most people's training load. Go Elite only if you genuinely plan to be on the mat most days.

03 Can I switch disciplines on the same tier?

Yes. On Basic and Intermediate you can switch which single martial art you're training at any time — just update it in your account. The change takes effect immediately, and you keep the same number of weekly sessions.

On Advanced you can swap either of your two disciplines whenever you like, and on Elite you have access to all four at all times.

04 What happens if I miss a week?

Nothing — your sessions don't roll over, but you're never penalised for missing class. If you're going to be away for a longer period, a membership freeze is the better option: billing stops and your tier is held until you're ready to come back.

05 Can I pay for a year up front?

Yes. Annual payment is available on any adult tier at a 10% discount — so Basic works out at £270 for the year instead of £300, and Elite at £648 instead of £720. Ask at reception or message us to set it up.

06 Is the fitness room included?

Yes — access to the fitness room is included at every membership tier, not just Elite. You don't need to book, and you can use it before class, after class, or on days when you're not training martial arts.

Non-members can also use the fitness room on a casual basis for £6.00 per visit.

07 Do I need to buy equipment?

Not straight away. We lend gis and gloves for your first few classes, so you can try a discipline before investing in your own kit. When you're ready to buy, we can recommend suppliers and most members order online.

A basic gi costs around £40–£60. Muay Thai gloves and wraps are around £40–£70 depending on brand.

08 Is there a discount for families or students?

Yes. Families with two or more junior members get 15% off the second and subsequent junior memberships. Students with a valid NUS or university card get 10% off any adult tier.

Discounts can be applied at signup or added later — just message us with proof of eligibility.

09 What payment methods do you accept?

All major debit and credit cards — Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Monthly memberships are billed automatically to the card on file. We don't currently accept direct debit or cash for recurring memberships, though casual fitness visits can be paid by card at reception.

10 Can I cancel online, or do I have to call?

You can cancel entirely online from your account page — no phone call, no form, no retention conversation. Cancellation takes effect from your next billing date, and you keep access until then.

If you'd rather talk it through first, a coach is always happy to help you find a tier that fits better.
