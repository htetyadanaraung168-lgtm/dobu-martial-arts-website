04 Payments

Billing and
payments.

Memberships are billed monthly by card on the date you joined. No direct debit, no minimum term, no exit fee.

Monthly

Automatic billing

Charged automatically to the card on file, on the same date each month. You'll get an email receipt every time.

Annual

Pay yearly, save 10%

Available on any adult tier. Basic works out at £270 for the year instead of £300; Elite at £648 instead of £720. Ask us to switch you.

No fees

Free to change

Switch tiers, freeze, or cancel entirely from your account page. No admin charges, no exit fees, no phone calls.

Membership freeze

Take a break without cancelling.

If you're injured, travelling or just need a break, you can freeze your membership. Your tier is held, your billing stops, and you pick up exactly where you left off.

  • Basic & Intermediate — 1 month per year
  • Advanced — 2 months per year
  • Elite — 3 months per year

05 Settings

Account
settings.

Keep your details up to date so we can reach you about gradings, timetable changes and anything else important. You can also control what we send you.

Why it matters

Keep your details current.

We only contact you when it matters — grading dates, class cancellations, membership changes. Having the right email and phone number means you never miss something important.

  • Grading dates and reminders
  • Timetable changes and cancellations
  • Payment receipts and billing notices
  • Event invitations and seminars
  • Emergency contact in case of injury

You can turn off marketing emails and keep essential account notices. We never share your details with third parties.

Update your details

Changes save immediately.

You'll get a confirmation email for sensitive changes.

Medical notes are only visible to coaches and reception. They're never shared with other members, and they help us keep you safe during training.

Close your account

Irreversible.

Closing your account permanently deletes your training history, attendance records and booking data. Your membership will also be cancelled. This cannot be undone.

If you just need a break, freeze your membership instead — you'll keep your account and your history.

06 Questions

Account
questions.

Common questions about accounts, memberships, billing and changes. If yours isn't here, get in touch and a coach will answer it directly.

01 Do I need an account to train at DoBu?

No — your first class is free and you don't need an account to book it. Just turn up ten minutes early in loose clothing, and a coach will take it from there.

You only need an account once you decide to join. An account is what lets you choose a membership, track your sessions, book private tuition and post on the forum.

02 Can I change my membership tier?

Yes, any time, from your account page. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades and cancellations take effect from your next billing date.

There's no fee for changing tiers, and no minimum term — you're only ever committed to the current month.

03 What happens if my payment fails?

We'll email you straight away and retry the payment three days later. If it fails a second time, we'll contact you directly to update your card.

Your membership stays active during this window — we won't cut off access over a failed payment without giving you a chance to fix it.

04 Can I pause my membership?

Yes — you can freeze your membership rather than cancelling. Your tier is held, billing stops, and you pick up exactly where you left off when you're ready to come back.

Freeze allowances are: Basic and Intermediate — 1 month per year; Advanced — 2 months; Elite — 3 months.

05 How do I cancel?

Entirely online, from your account page. No phone call, no form, no retention conversation. Cancellation takes effect from your next billing date, and you keep access until then.

If you're not sure, message us first — sometimes a freeze or a tier change is a better fit than cancelling.

06 Is my data safe?

Yes. Payment details are handled by a PCI-compliant payment processor — we never see or store your full card number. Medical notes are visible only to coaches and reception, and are never shared with other members.

We don't sell or share your data with third parties. You can close your account and delete your data at any time from the settings section.

07 Can I pay for a year up front?

Yes. Annual payment is available on any adult tier at a 10% discount — Basic works out at £270 for the year instead of £300, and Elite at £648 instead of £720.

Message us or ask at reception to switch to annual billing.

08 What if I forget my password?

Click the "Forgot?" link on the login form and we'll email you a reset link. The link is valid for one hour.

If you don't receive it within a few minutes, check your spam folder, or message us and we'll reset it manually.

09 Can I share my membership with a family member?

No — memberships are personal and can't be shared or transferred. That said, we do offer family discounts: 15% off the second and subsequent junior memberships for families with multiple children training.

Students with a valid NUS or university card get 10% off any adult tier.
