03 Your first class

What happens
on a trial.

Your first class is free, in any discipline, with no card and no obligation to join. Here's exactly what to expect so you can walk in knowing what's coming.

01

Arrive ten minutes early

Come in loose clothing — a t-shirt and tracksuit bottoms are ideal. Mention at reception that it's your first time and a coach will show you around, introduce you to a training partner, and lend you any equipment you need.

02

Join the warm-up

You'll join the class from the start — no sitting on the side. The warm-up is scaled so beginners can keep up, and the coach will explain everything as it happens. If you need a break at any point, just take one.

03

Train, then decide

You'll do the full class, including technique work and drills. Sparring is optional on a trial — you won't be thrown into anything you're not ready for. Afterwards, talk to the coach about which membership fits your week. Or don't. No pressure either way.

What to bring

The short list.

  • Loose clothing T-shirt and tracksuit bottoms. No zips, buckles or hard seams.
  • A water bottle There's a refill point by reception.
  • A towel Plus a change of clothes if you're heading somewhere afterwards.
  • Nothing else Gis, gloves and pads are all loaned free for your first few classes.

Remove all jewellery before stepping on the mat, including rings, watches and earrings. Keep fingernails and toenails trimmed short.

No commitment

Try before you decide.

Your first class is free and there's no follow-up sales pitch. If it's not for you, that's fine — you're welcome to try a different discipline before making a decision.

04 Questions

Contact
questions.

Common questions about getting in touch, booking trials and visiting the gym. If yours isn't here, send us a message above and a coach will answer directly.

01 How quickly will you reply to my enquiry?

Usually the same day, and almost always within 24 hours. If you send a message overnight, expect a reply first thing the following morning.

If your enquiry is urgent — you're already on your way, or you need to cancel a private session the same day — call 01632 960 482 and a coach will pick up during gym hours.

02 Do I need to book a trial class in advance?

No — you can just turn up to any class on the timetable for a free trial. Mention at reception that it's your first time and a coach will look after you.

That said, sending a message first is useful if you have questions, want a recommendation on which discipline to start with, or would like the coach to expect you.

03 Can I just come and watch a class before joining?

Absolutely. You're welcome to come and watch any session from the seating area before deciding. There's no obligation and nobody will approach you to sign up.

That said, most people find it easier to just join a free trial — you'll learn far more in ten minutes on the mat than an hour watching.

04 What should I wear to my first class?

Loose, comfortable clothing you can move in — a t-shirt and tracksuit bottoms are ideal. Avoid anything with zips, buckles or hard seams, since you'll be in close contact with other students.

We lend gis and gloves free for your first few classes, so you don't need to buy any equipment before trying a discipline.

05 Is there parking at the gym?

Yes — free on-street parking on Foundry Lane, both sides. It's busiest between 17:00 and 18:30 on weekdays, so arrive a few minutes early if you're coming to an evening class.

There's additional parking on Wharf Road, about a four-minute walk away, and bicycle racks at the gym entrance.

06 Can I book a kids' trial separately?

Yes. Select "Kids' classes" in the enquiry form and mention your child's age in the message. Kids' trials run in the same 15:00–17:00 slot as the regular junior classes, Monday to Friday.

Parents are welcome to watch from the seating area for the first session, and Sarah, Guy or Mauricio will take your child through the basics at their pace.

07 Can I visit the gym before deciding on a membership?

Yes — come and have a look around any time during gym hours. Reception can show you the matted area, the fitness room, the changing rooms, sauna and steam room, and answer any questions about the timetable.

If you want to use the fitness room on a casual basis while you decide, that's £6.00 per visit with no membership required.

08 What if I need to cancel a private tuition booking?

Just let us know as soon as you can — ideally at least 24 hours in advance. There's no cancellation charge, but the coach's time is booked specifically for you, so as much notice as possible helps.

You can reschedule to a different time at no cost, or cancel entirely and book again later.

09 Do you have changing facilities and showers?

Yes. Full changing rooms with lockers and showers, available before and after every session. Towels are available at reception, and the sauna and steam room are open through all gym hours for members.

Lockers are free to use — bring your own padlock if you'd like to secure your belongings.

10 Is the gym accessible?

The gym is on the ground floor with step-free access from Foundry Lane. There's an accessible toilet and changing facility, and a seating area next to the mat.

If you have specific access requirements, mention them in the enquiry form or call ahead and we'll make sure everything's ready when you arrive.
