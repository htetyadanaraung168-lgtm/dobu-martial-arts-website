04 Belt progression

How gradings
work.

Gradings happen twice a year, in spring and autumn. Your coach will tell you when you're ready, and put you forward — you don't need to ask.

On the day

What to expect.

Grading day is a formal session, but it's not meant to be intimidating. You'll be assessed on what you've already been practising in class, by coaches who know where you are.

  • Arrive 30 minutes early to warm up properly
  • Wear a clean, correctly tied gi with your current belt
  • Bring water and a spare t-shirt for between sessions
  • Judo and Jiu-Jitsu gradings include sparring rounds
  • Karate gradings include kata performance in front of the panel
  • Results are announced at the end of the day, on the mat

If you're not sure whether you're ready, ask your coach — they'll tell you honestly, and they'd rather you graded a session late than a session too soon.

07 Questions

Community
questions.

Everything about the forum, events, gradings and social channels. If yours isn't here, message us and we'll answer directly.

01 Do I need an account to read the forum?

No — the forum is publicly readable. Anyone can browse threads and announcements without signing in.

You will need an account to post a thread or reply to one. Accounts are free and take about two minutes to create from the My Account page.

02 How are forum posts moderated?

Lightly. The forum is member-run and self-moderating in practice — people are respectful, and there's very little that needs removing. That said, we'll remove anything that's abusive, unsafe, or that gives bad advice about training with an injury.

If you see something you think shouldn't be there, report it to a coach or message us through the contact page.

03 Do I have to attend gradings if my coach puts me forward?

No — being put forward is an invitation, not an obligation. If you're not ready, you're injured, or you simply can't make the date, just tell your coach. There'll be another grading in six months.

Coaches would rather you graded a session late than a session too soon. There's no pressure to accept.

04 Are events free for members?

It varies. Seminars and masterclasses have a small fee that's discounted for members. The inter-club friendly is free to enter and free to watch. The end-of-year social has a ticket price that covers food.

Every event listing on this page shows the cost and whether members get a discount.

05 Can I bring a guest to events?

For seminars and masterclasses — yes, non-members are welcome to book a place at the standard rate. It's a good way to bring a friend along without them committing to a full membership.

For the inter-club friendly — spectators are welcome, but only registered members can spar on the day.

For the end-of-year social — yes, members can bring a partner or friend. Just book their ticket when you book yours.

06 How do I get my grading result if I miss the announcement?

Results are announced on the mat at the end of the grading day, and posted on the forum within 48 hours. Your belt and certificate are held at reception until you collect them, and your grade is updated in your account.

07 Can I post about kit for sale or looking for a sparring partner?

Yes, both are welcome. Members regularly post about kit they're selling, gear they're looking for, and sparring partners for open mat or private drilling. Just keep it relevant to training at DoBu.

We don't allow commercial advertising from outside businesses without prior agreement — message us first if you're not sure.

08 Is there a WhatsApp or group chat?

There are informal WhatsApp groups per discipline — Judo, Jiu-Jitsu, Karate and Muay Thai. They're set up and run by members, not by the gym, and are useful for arranging open mat sessions and lift shares to competitions.

Ask a coach or a senior student if you'd like to be added to one.

09 How do I suggest an event or seminar?

Post it on the forum, or speak to Mauricio directly. We're always open to suggestions — a lot of what's on the events calendar now came from member ideas originally.

If there's a coach from outside the gym you'd like us to bring in for a seminar, let us know. We try to run one visiting-instructor seminar per year.
