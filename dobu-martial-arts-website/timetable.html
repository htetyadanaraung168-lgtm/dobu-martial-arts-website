03 Junior programme

Kids' classes,
five days a week.

Judo, Jiu-Jitsu and Karate all run dedicated junior sessions. Kids train separately from adults, in age-appropriate groups, with the same coaches who teach the adult classes.

Juniors are grouped by age and grade within each session, so a seven-year-old beginner isn't training at the same intensity as a fourteen-year-old with three years' experience.

What kids' classes cover

  • Fundamentals first Balance, coordination, footwork and safe falling, taught through games for younger groups.
  • Belt progression Juniors grade through the same belt system as adults, with a formal grading twice a year.
  • Respect and etiquette Bowing, dojo manners and looking after training partners are part of every session.
  • No pressure to spar Contact is introduced gradually and only when a child is ready and willing.

06 Questions

Timetable
questions.

Common questions about scheduling, attendance and booking. If yours isn't here, get in touch and we'll answer it directly.

01 Do I need to book group classes in advance?

No. Coached group classes and open mat are drop-in — just check the timetable, arrive ten minutes before the start time, and sign in at reception. There's no capacity limit that requires booking.

The only session that needs booking ahead is private tuition, which is arranged directly with the coach.

02 Can I attend classes in more than one discipline?

It depends on your membership tier:

  • Basic — one martial art, two sessions per week
  • Intermediate — one martial art, three sessions per week
  • Advanced — any two martial arts, five sessions per week
  • Elite — unlimited classes across all four disciplines

Open mat is included at every tier, so even on Basic you can come in and drill techniques from other arts during open mat time.

03 What happens if I miss a class?

Nothing — there's no penalty for missing a session. Your weekly allowance simply doesn't roll over. If you're on a limited tier and want to make up a missed class, you can usually attend a different session in the same week as long as it's within your discipline allowance.

04 Are the 06:00 classes suitable for beginners?

Yes. All early-morning classes are open to every level, including complete beginners. They tend to be quieter and slightly more focused than the evening sessions, which some people find easier for a first class.

Just be aware that you'll be training before eating for most people — bring water and take the warm-up seriously.

05 Why are there two-hour kids' classes but 90-minute adult classes?

The junior sessions run for two hours because they include a longer structured warm-up, more frequent breaks, and a mix of game-based and technical work suited to younger attention spans. The actual training time is comparable to an adult class — it's just paced differently.

06 Is there a class I can watch before joining?

Absolutely. You're welcome to come and watch any session from the seating area before deciding. That said, most people find it easier to just join a free trial class — you'll learn far more in ten minutes on the mat than an hour watching.

07 Do classes run on bank holidays?

We run a reduced timetable on bank holidays — typically the morning and early evening classes only, with private tuition slots cancelled. Any changes are posted on the community page and on our social channels at least a week in advance.

08 Can I switch disciplines partway through a month?

Yes. You can change your membership tier at any time from your account, and the change takes effect from your next billing date. If you want to switch immediately, speak to reception and they can usually adjust it on the spot.
