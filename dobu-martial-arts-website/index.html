01 The Gym

One membership.
The whole floor.

DoBu isn't a martial arts studio bolted onto a gym, or a gym with a mat in the corner. It's built as one floor — warm up on the fitness equipment, spar on the mats, then recover in the sauna or steam room, all under the same roof.

03 Why DoBu

Three things we
don't compromise on.

Not mission-statement filler. These are the principles that shape how classes are run, who gets hired, and what gets turned down.

01 / Principles

Beginners come first

Every class on the open timetable is designed so someone walking in for the first time can take part fully. If a session can't be taught safely to a complete beginner, it doesn't belong on the grid.

02 / Culture

Technique over ego

Sparring is a learning tool, not a proving ground. Coaches will stop a round if it becomes about winning rather than improving, and students who can't control their contact don't keep sparring.

03 / Access

No contracts, no upsells

Rolling monthly memberships. Free first class. Mat, gym, sauna and steam room included at every tier — not just the expensive one. Cancel any time online, with no fees and no phone call.
